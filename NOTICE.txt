ColorCramer
===========

ColorCramer is adapted from ColorBrewer (https://colorbrewer2.org/). The
diagnostic map, map overlays, terrain image, interface layout and descriptive
texts derive from the ColorBrewer web application. The ColorBrewer colour
schemes themselves have been replaced by Fabio Crameri's Scientific Colour Maps.

This product includes color specifications and designs developed by Cynthia
Brewer (http://colorbrewer.org/).

Scientific Colour Maps
----------------------

Colour maps by Fabio Crameri, MIT licence. If you use them, please cite:

  Crameri, F. (2018). Scientific colour maps. Zenodo.
  https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.1243862

  Crameri, F., Shephard, G.E. and Heron, P.J. (2020). The misuse of colour in
  science communication. Nature Communications, 11, 5444.

The browser-ready palette data comes from
https://github.com/justdan96/fabio-crameri-palette-generator
